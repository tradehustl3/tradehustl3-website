CREDENTIAL CHECK
OSHA explains that its Outreach Training Program issues course-completion cards. An OSHA 10-hour or 30-hour card is not a certification or license. Describe completed training accurately on your resume and check the requirements of the employer or jobsite you're approaching.
Source: https://www.osha.gov/training/outreach/faq
